# Evidence and publication method

## Purpose

This repository is a public case index, not a raw dump. It gives an independent reviewer a stable article, claim map, chronology, evidence fingerprints, and a clear boundary around what is and is not being asserted.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Primary bytes:** raw RFC822 email, official exports, exact conversation exports, saved logs.
2. **Exact projections:** readable text generated from primary material without replacing the source bytes.
3. **Owner-run reproductions:** controlled comparisons performed on the named accounts, clients, and routes.
4. **Personal testimony:** consequences and lived experience reported by the owner or family.
5. **Inference:** interpretation drawn from the above.

A summary, model response, support label, or self-authored receipt does not override the underlying primary source.

## Verification rules

- Every public hash is computed from the named private projection or manifest.
- Claims are labelled by evidence status.
- Correlation is not presented as private root cause.
- A support acknowledgement is not treated as a repair or legal admission.
- A sequence of family events is not presented as judicially established causation.
- Raw private material is released only through targeted, redacted review.

## Privacy boundary

The public repository excludes:

- email addresses and private identifiers;
- credentials, tokens, cookies, and payment data;
- raw family correspondence and medical material;
- private ChatGPT sessions and task content;
- raw logs containing unrelated work;
- data about third parties who did not consent to publication.

Do not paste private evidence into GitHub Issues. Contact the author through a private channel before requesting access.

## Independent review request

A reviewer should be able to ask for one claim, inspect its exact source location and hash, compare the redacted excerpt with the primary material, and see separately what the evidence does not prove.

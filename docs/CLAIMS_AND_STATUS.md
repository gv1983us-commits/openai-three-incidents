## Status vocabulary

- **DOCUMENTED:** present in a preserved source or exact correspondence.
- **OPENAI-ACKNOWLEDGED:** stated in an OpenAI response, without treating the response as proof of root cause.
- **REPRODUCED:** observed through a repeatable owner-run comparison.
- **OWNER-REPORTED:** reported by the customer but not independently reproduced in the public record.
- **OWNER-TESTIMONY:** personal account of consequences or lived experience.
- **INFERRED:** interpretation drawn from documented events.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** deliberately outside the public claim.

# Business incident

## Scope

This evidence family covers the ChatGPT Business incident from late September through October 2025.

The private primary set contains:

- 242 original RFC822/EML messages, preserved in sequence;
- the official OpenAI data export;
- an exact readable projection of the 242-message correspondence;
- supporting chronology and acceptance records held outside this public repository.

The public repository does **not** publish the raw EML, the official export, email addresses, payment details, private identifiers, or private family material.

## What this evidence supports

- a move from ChatGPT Plus to a two-seat Business workspace around September 29, 2025;
- a month-long period in which the workspace was reported as unreliable or unusable for the intended work;
- contemporaneous notice describing the newborn child, family obligations, loans, lost working time, and stalled business;
- repeated requests for a unified incident, a named owner, restoration, explanation, refund or compensation, and preservation of evidence;
- OpenAI support correspondence stating that reported bugs were recorded and acknowledged by technical teams;
- the absence in the preserved record of a delivered repair, accountable engineering owner, or requested refund/compensation;
- a late-October human response requesting a description, reproduction steps, and screenshots;
- a customer response supplying the requested material.

## What this evidence does not establish by itself

- OpenAI's private root cause;
- a court finding of breach, fraud, unlawful conduct, or damages;
- that the service was unavailable to every Business customer;
- that OpenAI directly caused the later family separation;
- the value of consequential or emotional damages.

The family sequence and the father's conclusion about its meaning are identified separately as owner testimony and inference.

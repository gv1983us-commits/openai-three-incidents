# ChatGPT Plus incident

## Scope

This evidence family contains fourteen exact ChatGPT conversation exports preserved as primary material. The exports were compared to test product boundaries rather than to treat model confidence as proof of execution.

The examination covered:

- conversation and project identity;
- memory and retrieval boundaries;
- file existence and persistence;
- tool execution;
- the difference between an interface statement and a material result;
- whether outputs survived outside the conversation that produced them.

Raw exports are not published in this repository. They may contain private conversations, identifiers, files, and internal project material.

## Export fingerprint

There are 14 exact primary exports. The manifest was computed from the ordered export directory name and the SHA-256 of each exact export:

The per-export hashes are retained in the private evidence manifest and can be supplied to qualified reviewers.

## What this evidence supports

- a controlled comparison of what the model claimed to know or do against what the product actually exposed;
- repeated checks of chats, files, tools, projects, memory, and surviving outputs;
- the distinction between a fluent model response and a verified completed action;
- the broader case question: whether the surrounding product reliably preserves the coordinates of a task.

## What this evidence does not establish by itself

- a universal defect affecting every Plus customer;
- OpenAI's private implementation or root cause;
- that every incorrect model statement was intentional;
- a legal conclusion about the Plus subscription;
- that model capability and product reliability are the same thing.

Targeted redacted excerpts can be supplied for a specific claim review. Raw conversation exports are not uploaded to the public repository.

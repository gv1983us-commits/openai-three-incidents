# Contact for journalists, lawyers, and researchers

This repository is a public index of a private evidence set concerning three paid OpenAI incidents:

- 242-message ChatGPT Business correspondence corpus;
- fourteen exact ChatGPT Plus conversation exports;
- Pro Full/Codex-Work incident logs and provider records.

## Requests

Qualified journalists, lawyers, and public-interest researchers may request targeted, redacted primary material for independent review.

A request should identify:

1. the claim or incident being reviewed;
2. the date range needed;
3. the purpose of the review;
4. a safe channel for receiving redacted material.

The complete raw corpus will not be posted in public issues or comments. It contains private correspondence, account identifiers, payment context, and family information. Material will be shared only to the extent needed for the stated review and with appropriate redaction.

## Review standard

The public case distinguishes:

- **DOCUMENTED** — directly present in preserved correspondence or exact exports;
- **REPRODUCED** — observed through a controlled comparison;
- **OPENAI_ACKNOWLEDGED** — acknowledged in an OpenAI response;
- **OWNER_TESTIMONY** — a personal account that requires independent corroboration;
- **INFERRED** — a conclusion drawn from the documented sequence;
- **NOT_ESTABLISHED** — not claimed as proven by this repository.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card data, authentication codes, or unrelated private records through GitHub.
